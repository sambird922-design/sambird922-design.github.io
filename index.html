Inspire Daily Clipper

A private, single-user tool that edits short videos and uploads them to two YouTube channels run by its owner, Sam Bird.

What it does

  • Edits short clips (framing, captions, cuts) on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads and schedules those videos to the owner's channels, Daily Truth Dose and The Beautiful Game.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags and thumbnail, and reads view statistics for those channels.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it never accesses anyone else's YouTube account or data.
