Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

Inspire Daily Clipper ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner, Sam Bird, to manage his own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel;
  • manage that channel's videos (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy and publish time);
  • read YouTube Analytics reports (views and watch statistics) for that channel.

How the data is used

Only to upload, schedule and update the owner's own videos, and to see how they perform so better videos can be made. The app does not access any other person's account, channel or data.

Storage and sharing

  • Google sign-in tokens and video statistics are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The data is kept only as long as the owner uses the app, and is deleted with it.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, or through Google's security settings page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the About page of Daily Truth Dose or The Beautiful Game.
